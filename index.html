Features

Built for the
field, not the lab.

📷Live camera scan

Point, capture, done. Photos are auto-resized so it works on slow 4G.

CameraUploadGemini vision
🌐7 languages, spoken

Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, English — with voice read-out for low-literacy users.

🧪Exact tank dose

Slide 5–25 L and see ml per tank. Acre mode counts how many tanks you need.

🌦️Is it safe to spray now?

Checks wind, rain chance and heat at your location and tells you to spray or wait.

WindRainHeat
📅Safe harvest date

Pick your spray date. We add the waiting period and show the first day it is safe to harvest.

🧤Icon-first safety

Gloves, mask, boots, no-wind — big pictures, not paragraphs.

📲Share on WhatsApp

Send the summary to workers or neighbours in one tap.

🕘Scan history

Your last labels are saved on your phone, even offline.
